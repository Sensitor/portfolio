## 💬 RESPONSES TO COMMON QUESTIONS

**Q: How is this different from Personal Capital / Mint?**
A: Those are account aggregators. We're analysis tools. They 
show you what you have. We show you what you SHOULD have.

**Q: Can I use this for crypto?**
A: Yes! Works with any asset on Yahoo Finance (stocks, ETFs, 
crypto, indices).

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes. We never store your actual holdings. Only the tickers 
and weights you input for analysis.

**Q: Do you offer financial advice?**
A: No. We're an analysis tool, not a financial advisor. All 
decisions are yours.

**Q: How accurate is the optimization?**
A: Based on Modern Portfolio Theory (Nobel Prize, 1990). Same 
math used by institutional investors. However, past performance 
doesn't guarantee future results.

**Q: Can I export reports?**
A: Yes! Pro users can export PDF reports.
